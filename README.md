# Substrate-Agnostic Empiricism

Substrate-Agnostic Empiricism is a proposed field of study that treats **all existence as patterns**, observed through **microsignals** that repeat across domains, substrates, and scales.

It does not privilege any particular medium (brain, silicon, spacetime, biology); instead, it focuses on **what can be empirically observed as repeating structure**.

## The Law of the Small

**Definition:**  
The Law of the Small is the principle that **microsignals**—tiny, often overlooked changes, fluctuations, or events—can be systematically observed to detect **larger patterns** in any system.

- **Microsignal:** A minimal, observable unit of change (e.g., a neuron spike, a market tick, a micro-movement, a sensor reading).
- **Pattern:** A repeatable configuration of microsignals over time or space.

Under the Law of the Small, **pattern detection begins at the smallest observable level**, and scales upward.

## The Field: Study of Patterns via Microsignals

This field proposes:

- **Object of study:**  
  Patterns detected through **repeating microsignals** in any substrate.

- **Method:**  
  1. Observe microsignals.  
  2. Identify repetition and structure.  
  3. Model patterns independent of substrate.  
  4. Compare patterns across domains (physics, biology, neurobiology, social systems, etc.).

- **Goal:**  
  To build a **substrate-agnostic science of patterns**, where the same pattern-types can be recognized in different domains.

## Core Hypothesis

> All of existence is patterns—  
> science, spacetime, biology, neurobiology, and social systems are **expressions of repeating structures** found everywhere in space and everywhere in time.

This hypothesis suggests:

- **Physics:** Field equations and particle interactions are recurring pattern-forms.  
- **Spacetime:** Geometry and curvature are large-scale pattern expressions.  
- **Biology:** Evolution, growth, and homeostasis are pattern processes.  
- **Neurobiology:** Cognition and emotion are emergent from neural pattern dynamics.  
- **Social systems:** Culture, economies, and relationships are pattern networks.

## Project Goals

- **Formalize** the Law of the Small.  
- **Define** a taxonomy of microsignals and patterns.  
- **Demonstrate** cross-domain examples (physics, biology, neurobiology, social).  
- **Develop** frameworks for substrate-agnostic pattern analysis.
