# Microsignal Taxonomy

A substrate-agnostic classification of microsignals used in pattern detection.

---

## 1. Temporal Microsignals
Small changes occurring over time.

Examples:
- neuron firing intervals  
- molecular reaction timing  
- market tick sequences  
- micro-behavioral shifts  

Used to detect:
- oscillations  
- cascades  
- feedback loops  

---

## 2. Spatial Microsignals
Small changes in position, geometry, or distribution.

Examples:
- micro-deformations in spacetime  
- cellular arrangement shifts  
- local clustering in social groups  

Used to detect:
- symmetry breaks  
- emergent structures  
- self-similarity  

---

## 3. Intensity Microsignals
Tiny changes in magnitude or strength.

Examples:
- voltage fluctuations  
- biochemical concentration changes  
- emotional micro-intensity shifts  

Used to detect:
- attractor states  
- stabilization loops  
- phase transitions  

---

## 4. Relational Microsignals
Small changes in relationships between elements.

Examples:
- synaptic weight adjustments  
- molecular binding affinity shifts  
- social alignment micro-events  

Used to detect:
- coherence  
- synchrony  
- emergent networks  

---

## 5. Frequency Microsignals
Changes in how often events occur.

Examples:
- spike rate changes  
- oscillation frequency shifts  
- repeated behavioral cues  

Used to detect:
- resonance  
- entrainment  
- harmonic structure  

---

## Summary
Microsignals fall into five universal categories:
- temporal  
- spatial  
- intensity  
- relational  
- frequency  

These categories apply across physics, biology, neurobiology, and social systems, supporting substrate-agnostic pattern analysis.
