# Glossary

Key terms used throughout Substrate-Agnostic Empiricism.

---

## Microsignal
A minimal, observable unit of change in any system.  
Examples: neuron spike, sensor fluctuation, micro-movement, market tick, geometric shift.

Microsignals become meaningful when they repeat.

---

## Pattern
A repeated configuration of microsignals across time or space.  
Patterns scale upward into larger structures (biological processes, physical laws, cognitive states).

---

## Substrate
The medium in which a pattern appears.  
Examples: neural tissue, molecules, spacetime geometry, computational systems, social behavior.

Substrate is secondary; patterns are primary.

---

## Pattern Equivalence
The observation that identical pattern types appear across different substrates.  
Example: feedback loops in biology, economics, and control systems.

---

## Law of the Small
The principle that microsignals reveal patterns earlier than macro-events.  
Small signals → repetition → pattern → structure.

---

## Pattern Taxonomy
A classification system for universal pattern types (oscillations, cascades, attractors, etc.) independent of domain.

---

## Substrate-Agnostic Empiricism
The scientific framework proposing that reality is composed of patterns detectable through repeating microsignals, regardless of substrate.
